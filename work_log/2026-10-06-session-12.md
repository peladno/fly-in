## 2. Architecture & Design Decisions

- **Partitioning Drones by Transit State:**
  - In `SimulationEngine.step()`, drones are split into `in_transit` (`d.is_in_transit`) and `waiting_drones` (`not d.is_in_transit and len(self.drone_targets[d]) > 0`).
  - *Rationale:* Ensures in-flight drones land and occupy destination capacity before stationary drones evaluate movement opportunities, preventing race conditions.

- **Phase 1 Arrival Resolution:**
  - For drones in `in_transit`, decrements `drone.steps_remaining`.
  - When `steps_remaining == 0`, adds drone to `dest_zone` (`drone.target_zone`), resets `target_zone = None`, pops destination from `drone_targets`, and records movement for turn reporting.
  - *Rationale:* Atomically finalizes arrivals without requiring re-checking of zone type or capacities at arrival time.

- **Phase 2 Departure Structure:**
  - Stationary drones are sorted by remaining path length (`waiting_drones.sort(key=lambda d: len(self.drone_targets[d]))`) to prioritize clearing bottlenecks.
  - Branches on `next_zone.zone_type != ZoneType.RESTRICTED` for instantaneous 1-turn moves vs `ZoneType.RESTRICTED` (transit departure).

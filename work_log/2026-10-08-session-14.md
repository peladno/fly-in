## 2. Architecture & Design Decisions

- **Simultaneous Connection Pipelining:**
  - Subject clarification: When a drone in transit lands on a restricted zone, it vacates the connection immediately. A subsequent drone can enter that connection on the very same turn.
  - Refined restricted takeoff criteria: changed from checking zone current occupancy directly to checking reserved inbound capacity (`inbound < limit`) and link availability (`link_available`).

- **Unified Movement Priority (`-0.5` Offset):**
  - Unified drone processing order in `SimulationEngine.step()`: sorted active drones by `len(self.drone_targets[d]) - (0.5 if d.is_in_transit else 0)`.
  - Drones closer to the destination move first so departing drones vacate zones and links for following drones in the same discrete turn.
  - Drones in transit landing this turn are effectively 0.5 steps ahead of stationary drones at the same waypoint distance, eliminating zone collisions.
  - Improved `02_circular_loop.txt` from 15 to 10 turns (benchmark $\le 15$), and `03_priority_puzzle.txt` from 9 to 7 turns (benchmark $\le 12$).

- **Visual Representation via ANSI Escapes:**
  - `MapParser` extracts `color` attribute from zone metadata (`[color=value]`).
  - `Zone` holds `color: str | None`.
  - `SimulationReporter` applies corresponding ANSI escape codes when initialized with `use_color=True`.
  - Added `--color` option to `src/main.py` and `visual` target to `Makefile`.

## 2. Architecture & Design Decisions

- **Custom Exception Hierarchy:**
  - Defined `MapParserError` as base exception, derived `InvalidSyntaxError`, `DuplicateZoneError`, `UnknownZoneError`, and `MissingHubError`.
  - *Rationale:* Allows clean, non-crashing error handling at top-level CLI while pinpointing line numbers and exact failure causes.

- **Modular Parser Design (SRP):**
  - Divided parser responsibilities:
    - `parse_file`: context manager and lifecycle.
    - `_parse_line`: line-level normalization and prefix routing.
    - `_parse_nb_drones`: drone integer validation.
    - `_parse_hub`: coordinate extraction and zone validation.
    - `_parse_metadata`: key-value extraction for zone types and capacity.
    - `_parse_connection`: edge linking and capacity extraction.
  - *Rationale:* Simplifies unit testing and keeps methods small and easily maintainable.

- **Strict Token & Constraint Validation:**
  - Enforced that zone names cannot contain `-` (reserved as connection delimiter).
  - Validated float coordinates and positive drone limits.
  - Provided `is_unlimited` flag on `_parse_hub` to enforce unbounded capacity for `start_hub` and `end_hub`.

## 5. Challenges, Blockers & Lessons Learned
- **Error Handling vs. Silent Printing:** Catching `ValueError` without re-raising or converting to domain exception results in implicit `None` returns; re-raising `InvalidSyntaxError` guarantees contract safety.
- **Line Number Propagation:** Passing `line_num` into helper methods enables descriptive error diagnostics.

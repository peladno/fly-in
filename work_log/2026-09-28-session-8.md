## 2. Architecture & Design Decisions

- **Turn Stepping Priority Order:**
  - Sorted active drones by remaining waypoints in ascending order: `active_drones.sort(key=lambda d: len(self.drone_targets[d]))`.
  - *Rationale:* Drones nearest to the destination advance first, immediately clearing intermediate zones and preventing false capacity deadlocks for trailing drones advancing within the same turn.

- **State Transition Atomicity in `step()`:**
  - For each eligible drone:
    1. Removes drone from current zone (`drone.current_zone.remove_drone(drone)`).
    2. Enters new zone (`next_zone.add_drone(drone)`).
    3. Updates internal zone pointer (`drone.current_zone = next_zone`).
    4. Pops front target from queue (`self.drone_targets[drone].pop(0)`).
    5. Appends tuple `(drone, next_zone)` for turn reporting.

- **Package Re-exporting:**
  - Exposed `SimulationEngine` and `SimulationReporter` from `src/simulation/__init__.py` with `__all__`.

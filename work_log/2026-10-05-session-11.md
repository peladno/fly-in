## 2. Architecture & Design Decisions

- **Full Subject Requirements Audit:**
  - Audited `flyin.pdf` across all mandatory chapters (VII.1 to VII.7):
    - *Movement mechanics:* Normal zones cost 1 turn; restricted zones cost 2 turns; priority zones cost 1 turn (preferred in routing).
    - *Restricted zone protocol:* Turn 1 outputs `D<ID>-<connection>` (e.g., `D1-loop_b-exit_point`), occupying link capacity; Turn 2 outputs `D<ID>-<zone>` (e.g., `D1-exit_point`).
    - *Constraint:* Drones cannot wait on connections; destination capacity must be guaranteed upon arrival.
    - *Visual representation:* Mandatory terminal color output or GUI displaying drone positions and zone states.
    - *Testing:* Unit tests in `tests/` covering edge cases.

- **Two-Phase Turn Execution in `SimulationEngine.step()`:**
  - **Phase 1 (Arrivals / Completing Transit):**
    - Process drones currently in flight (`drone.is_in_transit`).
    - Decrement `steps_remaining`; upon reaching 0, deliver drone to `target_zone`, pop target waypoint, update `current_zone`, and emit `D<ID>-<zone>`.
    - Drones completing arrival in Phase 1 do not initiate a second move in the same turn.
  - **Phase 2 (Departures / Starting Movement):**
    - Process stationary drones sorted by remaining waypoints.
    - For normal destinations: move immediately if `not next_zone.is_full()`, emit `D<ID>-<next_zone>`.
    - For restricted destinations: verify both link capacity and destination reservation (`current_occupancy + inbound_in_transit < max_drones`). If available, remove drone from current zone, set `steps_remaining = 1` and `target_zone`, and emit `D<ID>-<connection>`.

- **Capacity Reservation Model:**
  - Inbound transit drones are counted towards destination capacity (`sum(1 for d in drones if d.target_zone == zone)`).
  - *Rationale:* Strictly enforces the subject constraint prohibiting drones from stalling on connections if destination happens to be occupied upon arrival.

## 5. Challenges, Blockers & Lessons Learned
- **Connection Output Format:** Confirmed from the subject that during transit to restricted zones, output format requires the connection identifier string (`D<ID>-<zone_from>-<zone_to>`). `SimulationReporter` will need to accept either `Zone` or `str` for flexible emission.
- **Strict Turn Limits on Transit:** Because drones cannot pause or wait in flight, departure toward a restricted zone requires atomic reservation of the arrival slot.
